# Content and autonomy policy

Status — proposed operating rules to approve at G2.

Business names, prices, offers, opening hours and claims come from approved brand data. Generated text references the revision of those facts. Missing or conflicting facts become a visible question. Customer data and credentials are excluded from creative prompts unless the approved workflow requires specific data and permits its processing.

## Authority levels

| Mode | Permitted outcome | Approval evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | Create and revise content | User request or approved generation schedule |
| Approved publication | Publish an exact revision to a named account | Explicit content approval |
| Bounded autonomy | Generate and publish within a defined policy | Approved accounts, topics, formats, frequency, budget, expiry and escalation rules |

Policy changes are versioned. A worker checks current authority immediately before an irreversible action. Stopping or revoking authority prevents newly initiated writes. Editing content after approval requires a new approval or a policy that explicitly covers the resulting revision.

## Media and reputation

Record permission and source for uploaded images, music, voice and likeness. Generated media retains its provenance. Platform-specific labeling rules are part of the selected channel contract. Review replies must be based on the actual review and approved business facts. Sensitive complaints follow an escalation rule defined before autonomy is enabled.

MS-012 evaluates real outputs under these rules and records failure rates, cost and interventions before live autonomous use is approved.
