# MS-009 · Package G accepted pilot evidence map

**Scope.** One business, one customer-approved organic GBP text post and one occurrence; tenant B probes isolation. Owner decisions confirm text-only scope, customer roles, invitations, approval/export and the targeted administrator stop. The [owner decision](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/issues/15#issuecomment-6681723454) accepts the local mechanisms and bounds. For Manager approval, Employee invitation/removal and sensitive DEV/Admin actions, exercise proof age exactly 5 minutes (allowed) and greater than 5 minutes (denied) in the existing owning suites. Execution follows the decision-record merge and coordinator dispatch. All cases below are **planned and unexecuted**. [Pilot decision](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/issues/8#issuecomment-6662099692) · [Stop decision](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/issues/30#issuecomment-6670980326) · [Package G assignment](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/issues/9#issuecomment-6671186754)

**Fixtures and stages.** Use synthetic tenants A/B, a Manager and Employee per tenant, an existing identity shared across A/B for revocation tests, a separately enrolled Windows admin on a disposable VM, test identity service, controlled clock and counting GBP fake. Reset each scenario; bind approval to tenant/revision/location/timezone/instant/occurrence. At **G3 integration** (#10), record the resulting durable state, customer-visible status and fake calls. At **G3 staging** (#82), repeat applicable cases on the authorized artifact and owned surfaces before external access. A fake LIVE result proves only the local contract. G3 publication evidence needs a separately authorized, uniquely correlated Google-side observation. [Stage evaluations](../../08_EVALS.md) · [G2 work plan](2026-11-05-g2-work-plan.md)
