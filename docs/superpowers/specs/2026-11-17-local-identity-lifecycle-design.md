# Local identity lifecycle: source-based design disposition

**Status:** The coordinator accepted the browser/OIDC direction as a bounded draft on 2026-11-17; source-only preparation for [#131](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/issues/131). Not an accepted lifecycle implementation contract or completed authentication system. Base: `c7f19e4d02f64bafcc97e0fd6f80e714c711332b`. The [accepted G2 contract](2026-11-06-g2-acceptance-packet.md), [ACCESS requirements](../../17_ACCESS_SECURITY.md) and [Task 2 plan](../plans/2026-11-05-ms-009-local-text-pilot.md#2-customer-registration-portal-and-immutable-approval) remain binding.

## Recommendation and alternative

Keep Keycloak 26.8.2 responsible for passwords, factors and browser authentication; TypeScript/Node owns application admission, PostgreSQL owns local challenge/session/tenant transactions. Prefer supported authorization-code flows and provider-owned required actions over handling passwords in the application. **The inspected sources do not yet support the complete accepted signup/recovery contract.** Keep public authentication and completion production unavailable until the coordinator dispositions the missing guarantees below. This is a design direction, not permission to enable a partial lifecycle.

The serious alternative is entirely native Keycloak registration/recovery followed by application onboarding. It reduces custom lifecycle orchestration, but the [prior checkpoint](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/blob/f882d10a81df0dbe5a4366edca22d201110ac41c/docs/handovers/sessions/2026-11-17-customer-access-checkpoint.md) records native verification changing state on GET and retaining preregistration credentials. It therefore does not meet the accepted contract. Replacing verified subjects, a custom IdP/SPI, a new platform, or silently relaxing requirements are not alternatives authorized here.

## Minimum state and transaction boundary

Proposed application state is limited to the configured issuer/subject binding; hashed mailbox challenge with identity, purpose, version, generation, expiry and consumption/revocation; durable operation ownership/phase/outcome; and, only after a proven provider boundary, an opaque one-use completion reference. Reuse existing identity/session/membership storage. Store no passwords or factor secrets; keep tokens server-side and out of URLs/logs/application records where not needed. Do not trust email as the stable identity key.

Conceptual phases are `pending-proof`, `provider-pending`, `reconciliation-required`, `completed` and `cancelled`. Their exact transition protocol is **unresolved**. A unique current generation and compare/recheck under application locks can reject stale *local* commits; they cannot stop a later external credential write. A lost provider response/crash leaves an unresolved operation denied, never inferred complete after a delay. Compensation cannot restore old authority, delete/recreate a verified identity, or blindly repeat an uncertain credential operation. Durable reconciliation evidence is required before release.

The eventual completion must bind operation, immutable subject, purpose, exact challenge/invitation, identity version and current generation. Only proven provider completion may produce it. Its consumer must recheck these bindings and atomically consume it with the intended local grant. Signup yields exactly one new Employee workspace; invitation completion yields no independent role or workspace. `identity_operations`, `identity_completions` and `consumeIdentityCompletion` remain unfrozen; names from old exploratory code are not an API promise.

The current [#131 delivery requirement](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/issues/131) additionally says: “After completed recovery, stale links/forms/requests/credentials/sessions must not replace or regain the new authority.” This remains binding; it is not silently rewritten as session revocation only.

**Interpretation:** Both application-authority exclusion and effective credential integrity matter. The sources do **not** literally require that every internal IdP credential write cease forever after completion. That blanket implementation condition would be stronger than the stated product requirement. A late operation is disallowed when it restores usable old credentials/authority or displaces the promised owner's credential. An application-generation counter alone does not establish these postconditions; permanently denying everyone also fails a successful recovery's positive outcome. The unresolved obligation is a supported completion boundary that establishes these outcomes while preserving identity, not a mandated internal locking technique. Missing evidence is not proof that Keycloak cannot supply an adequate design.

**Coordinator disposition, 2026-11-17:** The interpretation above is accepted: usable credentials and authority are decisive, not a blanket ban on internal writes. A1's concrete credential ID is not a new product requirement. The preferred further direction is a provable issuer-signed authentication context with reliable freshness and post-recovery revocation; enrollment alone or an asserted claim is insufficient. This disposition authorizes these two documents only, with no A1 code change or unproven lifecycle implementation. Root decides the next delivery after #117.

## Reuse the #117 boundary

The inspected [A1 contract/plan at `badc7af`](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/blob/badc7af09f0a1c05883b957cd0f2e6ed115001f7/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-06-task-2-deliveries.md#a1-shared-contract) supplies `CustomerContext`, `InspectIdentity(subject)`, `customerRequest`, `requireCustomerRequest`, `requireManagerMfa`, `rotateCustomerSession` and local logout. Preserve caller-owned transactions and lock order: complete sorted identities, sorted tenants, memberships by tenant/id, sessions, domain records; revalidate preliminary bindings. Reconcile with the actual merged #117 before implementation.

`InspectIdentity` must become an authenticated, validated, fail-closed server adapter. Listing a current factor does not prove it was used. **An exact credential ID is A1's representation choice, not an accepted product requirement.** [Access L3](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/blob/c7f19e4d02f64bafcc97e0fd6f80e714c711332b/docs/17_ACCESS_SECURITY.md#L3) requires “enrolled MFA with proof age at most 5 minutes”; [L52](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/blob/c7f19e4d02f64bafcc97e0fd6f80e714c711332b/docs/17_ACCESS_SECURITY.md#L52) requires “Account recovery must restore MFA before these actions resume.” [Accepted Task 2 L24](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/blob/c7f19e4d02f64bafcc97e0fd6f80e714c711332b/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-05-ms-009-local-text-pilot.md#L24) requires authenticated enrollment, fresh proof and denial after recovery without restored MFA; it specifies no credential identifier.

**Preferred MFA variant:** validated issuer-signed authentication context with documented, configured enrolled-factor semantics, a trustworthy proof-time bound, and current enrollment/recovery generation. Keycloak's [step-up and authentication-flow sections](https://www.keycloak.org/docs/26.8.2/server_admin/#_step-up-flow) document LoA/ACR and AMR mapping. They also distinguish enrollment during a flow from authentication with that factor. Therefore context-based evidence can satisfy the product requirement without proving one credential ID, provided all relevant enrollment/recovery changes invalidate prior authority and fresh proof is actually established. An exact factor-ID mechanism remains an alternative if supported evidence warrants it.

**Remaining MFA gap:** the selected flow's claim/time semantics, reliable current enrollment/recovery invalidation and compatible A1 representation are not established here. `auth_time` must not simply be assumed to timestamp the second factor; neither callback receipt time, refreshed `iat`, arbitrary listed credential nor caller-supplied `amr` supplies proof. Coordinate any change to A1's `mfa_credential_id`/`mfa_at` with root; do not manufacture an ID or silently alter the interface. Recovery still requires reenrollment before sensitive actions.

## Unchanged acceptance and decision needed

- Explicit CSRF-protected challenge POST; GET performs no activation, verification, consumption or credential mutation. OIDC callback validation must not silently redefine this mailbox rule. Bind code/state/nonce/PKCE and fixed return targets; reject replay and mismatched issuer/audience/subject.
- Verification/invitation 24 hours; reset 15 minutes; single redemption; resend invalidation. Login limits 5/account and 30/source per 15 minutes; signup/recovery/resend each 3/account and 10/source per hour, including unknown identities. Direct IdP entry paths must not bypass accepted limits or public response equivalence.
- Required first/last names, optional remaining profile, IdP-owned user-chosen password. New/unverified activation removes preregistration credentials/factors/challenges/sessions. Existing verified identities retain subject, profile, credentials, factors and other memberships; duplicate signup gives equivalent public output and mailbox-only guidance. No domain joining or elevated role assignment.
- Customer idle 30 minutes/absolute 8 hours; revocation overrides expiry and reactivation cannot revive stale authority. MFA age exactly five minutes is allowed; future/stale proof is denied. Separate customer/admin authority persists. App logout denial is immediate; provider logout completion/uncertainty is recorded separately, following [OIDC logout](https://openid.net/specs/openid-connect-rpinitiated-1_0.html).

**Open proof obligations:** provider-supported identity-preserving completion satisfying the authority and credential postconditions above; trustworthy enrolled-MFA context/time/generation provenance; durable crash/retry/reconciliation semantics; end-to-end entry-path enforcement. No inspected source closes these. All #131 ACCESS-01/02/04/06/07/09/10/11/12/13/14/17 cases, positive controls, canary checks, keyboard signup-to-dashboard and current-candidate CI/Claude API review remain open. Existing synthetic results cannot close them. Root owns technical disposition; no repeated G2 approval is requested.
