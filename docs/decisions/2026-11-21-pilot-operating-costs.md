# Operating cost evidence for one Frankfurt pilot month

Prices and public terms checked on **2026-11-21** for [#178](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/issues/178). [ADR 0002](0002-google-text-pilot.md#what-would-one-pilot-month-cost) supplies the workload: one month, one tenant and location, one organic text post, one Windows administrator, an 8 GB Linux host with public IPv4, at most 1 GB of retained encrypted logical backups, under 25 GB of bucket transfer, low portal traffic and at most 100 small verification/recovery emails. The [accepted G2 scope](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/issues/15#issuecomment-6681723454) is local and synthetic. The costs below inform the separate operating decision in [#10](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/issues/10).

## Windows trust and recovery costs

The priced signing paths are alternatives for the same owner-controlled HTTPS MSIX feed. The released package must carry a certificate subject matching the package's publisher with a trusted chain, a valid timestamp and an accepted version on the enrolled Windows build and a clean recovery PC. Budget one initial and one corrective package signature. Private Trust needs a separate managed trust design. Test profiles, self-signed certificates and Store distribution do not establish the assumed trust and delivery path. ## Subtotals and owner decisions

- **USD 50.09 before tax**, illustrative recurring platform and usage subtotal: 44 + 1 + 4 + 0.008 + 0.016384 + 1.0687 = 50.093084. With annual domain renewal spread over 12 months for comparison, **USD 50.84**. The domain is charged annually. A month with the restore but no domain invoice is **USD 51.51 before tax**.
- **USD 60.51 before tax** for that month with one restore and a registration or renewal due: 50.093084 + 1.41912 + 9 = 60.512204. If AWS bills German VAT at its published 19%, that subtotal corresponds to **USD 72.01 including VAT**, before invoice-currency conversion, fees or rounding differences. [AWS EMEA VAT](https://aws.amazon.com/tax-help/emea-vat-tables/)
- If the Azure Basic path qualifies at the published USD rate, the comparable pre-tax list-price subtotal becomes **USD 70.50** with one billed month of signing. It still assumes Basic AWS support. Budget **EUR 67.75 including VAT and German delivery** separately for the keys. The alternative EUR 209 annual signing reference has unresolved tax and is separate from both currencies' subtotals.
- Operator labour and support remain open. For comparison only, model 4 hours setup/verification + 2 hours routine operations + 4 hours restore drill + 2 hours shutdown/evidence = **12 operator-hours × an owner-approved hourly rate**, with 2 hours as the recurring one-month allowance. On-call, incident response, vendor coordination and any contractor minimum are unpriced. Confirm the actual operator arrangement and include any quote or internal time allocation separately.

Maxi owns the decision in [#10](https://github.com/Maeximilion/MaexSocial/issues/10). Record the legal payer and billing currency, start/end and renewal period, absolute cap per billing currency (including taxes, shipping, annual charges and retained resources), escalation and stop authority with someone able to act when the primary operator is unavailable, and named owners for patching, backup/restore and key recovery. Confirm vendors, plan, terms, regions and transfers for host, identity, backup, mail, logs and signing, domain ownership, and actual signer eligibility. Price any independently retained audit or recovery components after their design is agreed. Recheck official rates before approval and immediately before any commitment, and repeat if the payer, vendor, region, plan, workload, retention, approvals or dates change. The public figures leave an all-in cap and external operation pending owner approval and tested spend controls.
